# Friday 8/28 — 120 min

**Sidelines 0–30, half court 30–120.** Friday's floor, so the half-court hour and a half is
six 15-minute blocks: **three drills, each one immediately followed by the game that tests
it.** Nothing gets a constraint tonight that didn't get drilled ten minutes earlier.

**Small-sided all night — 3v3, or 3v2 if we're short.** Six or more and it's 3v3. Five and it's
**3v2**, which is the better problem anyway: the defense is a man short by design, so somebody
is always open and the ball has to find him. That's the same lesson the 7-pass rule is after.

**Every scrimmage is a constrained game.** [Tonight's constraints](#tonights-constraints) lists
all of them and which block each one goes live in. **Bold marks what's new in that block;
plain text is carried over.**

**Substitutions run off the last bucket. Track who scored last** —
[substitutions](#substitutions--the-last-bucket-is-the-sub).

## Tonight's constraints

Every one of them gets drilled before it gets enforced. **Where it goes live** is the block it
first counts in — after that it stays on.

**Offense — the penalty is a turnover**

| Constraint | Goes live |
|---|---|
| **Spread on the inbound**, and the ball crosses the clear line **by pass only** | Scrimmage 1 |
| Out of **SL/SR** — every possession runs a **downscreen or backscreen** | Scrimmage 1 |
| Out of **3-Out** — every possession runs a **P'n'R or screen away** | Scrimmage 2 |
| **7 passes before anyone can score** | Scrimmage 2 |
| **No back-to-back scoring by the same player** — his next bucket doesn't count | Scrimmage 3 |

**Defense — the penalty is a free throw and the offense keeps the ball**

| Constraint | Goes live |
|---|---|
| **Box out on every shot attempt** | Scrimmage 3 |

**The clear line** is the half-court line. On any change of possession or dead ball the ball
goes back behind it and comes in **by pass** — nobody dribbles it out. Same rule we ran full
court on Tuesday, just a shorter floor.

## The call — 3-Out, SL, SR

**The PG calls the offense out loud before the defense is set.** One word, one time, loud
enough that everyone hears it.

- **3-Out** — neutral. Three across the perimeter, paint empty, no side declared. **Tonight
  3-Out means P'n'R or screen away.**
- **SL** — strong side left. The ball goes left and the action happens left. **Tonight SL/SR
  means downscreen or backscreen.**
- **SR** — strong side right. Mirror image.

**The call now tells them which screen to look for.** That's the point of pairing them — the
side isn't just where the ball goes, it's which half of the playbook is live. Full 4v4
breakdown of 5-Out and Motion off the call is in
[Friday 8/21's plan](26-08-21.md#4v4--slsr-into-5-out-or-motion).

## Substitutions — the last bucket is the sub

**Keep one fact in your head all night: who scored last.**

- **At the next dead ball, the last scorer and the man guarding him come off.** Two fresh
  bodies go on. Nobody gets singled out and the game never stops for a lecture.
- **Back-to-back buckets by the same player → he comes off immediately.** In the last game
  that's already a rule with a penalty attached, so the sub is automatic.
- **A penalty is also a substitution.** The player who broke the rule comes off; the free
  throw is when I tell him *which rule he broke*, not how to beat it. He goes back in at the
  next stoppage once he's said the rule back to me.
- **In 3v2 nobody sits** — five on the floor, and the odd man rotates onto defense every
  possession so the short side is never the same two players.
