# Tuesday 8/25 — 120 min

**Full court 0–60, half court 60–90, sidelines 90–120.** We have the whole floor for the first
hour, so the full court gets the hour and everything that needs it goes there. **No warm-up
block up front — the hour is four 15-minute blocks and nothing else.** Dribbling and
stretching run in the sidelines block at the end, where they don't cost us floor time.

**Three drills, then the press pair runs backwards.** Free throws first while every basket in
the gym is free — it's also the currency every defensive penalty pays out in tonight, so the
rep and the stakes are the same thing. **It's the only easy block on the floor, so get them
moving on their own before the first ball goes up** — the Hail Mary is a full-speed sprint
ten minutes in.

Then they play a pressed game **cold**, find out what it costs, and *only then* do we drill
the break. The fix lands on a problem they've already felt instead of one I described, and
it's the last thing they do on the full court — so the answer is the freshest thing in their
heads when they leave the floor.

**Friday's calls carry over and don't retire.** The PG calls the offense — **3-Out, SL, SR** —
crossing mid-court, and a silent possession is a turnover. What's new tonight is that the ball
has to *survive* a press to get there. [Recap of the calls](#the-call--3-out-sl-sr).

**Substitutions run off the last bucket. Track who scored last** —

## The call — 3-Out, SL, SR

**The PG calls the offense out loud as he crosses mid-court, before the defense is set.** One
word, one time, loud enough that everyone hears it.

- **3-Out** — neutral. Three across the perimeter, paint empty, no side declared. Read the
  defense and play.
- **SL** — strong side left. The ball goes left and the action happens left.
- **SR** — strong side right. Mirror image.

**SL/SR declares the side, not the play.** What happens inside the side is still a read — full
4v4 breakdown of 5-Out and Motion off the call is in
[Friday's plan](26-08-21.md#4v4--slsr-into-5-out-or-motion).

## Substitutions — the last bucket is the sub

**Keep one fact in your head all night: who scored last.**

- **At the next dead ball, the last scorer and the man guarding him come off.** Two fresh
  bodies go on. Nobody has to be singled out and the game never stops for a lecture.
- **Back-to-back buckets by the same player → he comes off immediately**, dead ball or not.
- **A penalty is also a substitution.** The player who broke the rule comes off; the free
  throw is when I tell him *which rule he broke*, not how to beat it. He goes back in at the
  next stoppage once he's said the rule back to me.
- 9 athletes: **4v4 leaves one off, 3v3 leaves three off.** The odd man goes to the stronger
  side, so I have a bench on that side only and the weaker team never leaves the floor. In the
  3v3 press game the three off are the next team on — rotate them in on the last bucket.
