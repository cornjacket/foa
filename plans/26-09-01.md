# Tuesday 9/1 — 120 min

> **Written for 6 athletes.** Six is the number where 3v3 works perfectly and **nobody ever
> comes off the floor.** If we get four, this plan doesn't run — see [if we get
> four](#if-we-get-four).

**Full court 0–60, half court 60–90, sidelines 90–120.**

**Six means no bench, and no bench means no substitutions.** Every lever I normally have —
pull the scorer, sit the kid who broke the rule, rotate a fresh body in — is gone tonight.
**What replaces it is the clock and the water bottle.** Blocks alternate hard and easy on
purpose, and every block change is a water break, taken whether they ask for it or not. Six
kids playing full-court 3v3 with no relief get sloppy around minute nine, and sloppy is when
somebody turns an ankle.

**The two dribble rules go in tonight** — they've been queued since 8/25 and they're the same
idea twice: **the ball moves by pass until somebody actually has an advantage.** They get
drilled in the half-court block before the game enforces them.

**Bold marks what's new in that block; plain text is carried over.**

## The call — 3-Out, SL, SR

**The PG calls the offense out loud before the defense is set.** One word, one time.

- **3-Out** — neutral. Three across the perimeter, paint empty. **3-Out means P'n'R or screen
  away.**
- **SL** — strong side left. **SL/SR means downscreen or backscreen.**
- **SR** — strong side right. Mirror image.

At 3v3 the call is the whole offense — there's no fourth man to bail anybody out, so the side
declaration and the action are all the structure there is.

## Substitutions — there aren't any tonight

**Six athletes, 3v3, nobody sits.** The whole substitution system is off. Say so out loud at
the start so nobody spends the night waiting for a rotation that isn't coming.

**What replaces it:**

- **Every block change is a water break** — not optional, not "if you need it." Six kids with
  no relief will run themselves into sloppy, and sloppy is how somebody gets hurt.
- **The penalty conversation happens at the free throw**, the same as always — the shooter's
  at the line, so it costs the game nothing. He just doesn't come off after it.
- **The load column is the plan.** Hard blocks are followed by easy or medium ones on purpose.
  If a hard block is falling apart, end it early and take the next one — the schedule is a
  guide, not a debt.
- **Back-to-back scoring already has a penalty** — that rule is doing the job the sub rule
  normally does, keeping one kid from taking over.

## If we get four

plan, not a trimmed version of this one: 2v2 full court, 2v1 advantage work, and 1v1 in the
