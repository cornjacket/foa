## Half court (7:45–9:00)

### 7:45 — Water and huddle (2)

### 7:47 — Form shooting: close, mid, far (7)

**Perfect form. No sloppy 3s — stay in your range.** Make a few from close, then move to mid, then
far. A player's far spot is the edge of their own range, not the 3-point line.

### 7:54 — Quick shooting off a pass (6)

**Chest-level pass, quick shot, with a closeout.** The passer closes out on the shooter after every
pass. Shooter and passer swap on every rep.

### 8:00 — 1v1 to 5 points (6)

**Game to 5. The left hand wins the game:** the winning point must be a left-hand finish. The group
of 3 plays winner stays.
