# Friday 10/9 — 90 min

**Outdoor hard court, 6:00–7:30.** Go easy on the concrete: no scrimmaging, no full-speed offense vs
defense, no sprints. **Tonight is 3-Out, Flex 1 and 2, and shooting.**

**Ask for volunteers for Saturday's 7th/8th grade tournament** at the opening huddle, and collect
names before anyone leaves.

## 6:00 — Huddle: Saturday volunteers (3)

Ask who can play in **Saturday's 7th/8th grade tournament**. Get hands up now and confirm names at
7:25.

## 6:03 — Warm-up + dribbling (12)

Easy jog, then hips, hamstrings, groin, calves. **Dribbling at half speed, down and back**, so the
legs are warm without pounding.

**Routines:** between the legs · under drags · cross jabs · speed stops

## 6:15 — Shooting drill: pass, pump, drive left, elbow jumper (15)

**3 lines of 3**, from the wings. Coach or the next man in line passes.

1. **Catch** on the wing, square up
2. **Pump fake** — sell it with the ball and the eyes, not the feet
3. **Drive left**, one or two dribbles to the elbow
4. **Pull up**: jump shot

Shooter gets his own rebound and goes to the back of the next line.

**Last 5 minutes, add the option: pump-n-step-thru.** After the pump, step through past the defender
instead of pulling up, and finish. **Shooter chooses** — pull-up or step-thru — so it is a read,
not a script.

## 6:30 — 3-Out: downscreen and motion (15)

**The 1-3-1 we introduced with Flex.** Offense only. Walk it, then bring it up to speed.

- **3-Out (downscreen)**
- **3-Out (motion)**

Run both from the same look so the shape doesn't tell the defense which one is coming. Five on the
floor, rotate the rest in after every rep.

## 6:45 — Flex 1 and Flex 2 out of 3-Out (15)

Same 1-3-1 start.

1. **Flex 1**
2. **Flex 2**

**Run each until it has been scored**, then switch sides. Full version of the formation is in
[9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 7:00 — Run through all four (10)

**Coach calls it, the PG repeats it out loud, they run it.** 3-Out (downscreen) · 3-Out (motion) ·
Flex 1 · Flex 2, in random order. Finish every rep with a shot.

**Optional: two light defenders on the wings, hands only, no contest.** That makes the wings get
open without a full-speed rep on the concrete.

## 7:10 — Shooting (15)

- **Pair up: free throws** (5) — make 2 in a row, then swap
- **3-pt competition** (10)

## 7:25 — Huddle: confirm Saturday (5)

**Get the names of everyone who can play Saturday**, and tell them the time and the location.
