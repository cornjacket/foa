# Tuesday 8/11 — 120 min

**Last Tuesday's framework, same court blocking: full court 0–60, half court 60–90,
sidelines 90–120.** The sideline half-hour is 8/4's, unchanged — stationary bank, combos,
1v1 containment, 2v2 press-break inbounding. What's new is the whole first hour.

**Today's through-line: read the defense before you run anything.** Man or zone isn't a
scouting note, it's a decision the point guard makes out loud in the first three seconds of a
possession, and the answer changes what we run:

- **Man → screen.** A man defender has to follow his man, so a screen actually costs him
  something.
- **Zone → flood and crash.** Zone defenders guard air, so **overload one side** until there
  are more of us than them in it — and **crash the glass**, because in a zone nobody has been
  assigned a body to box out. That's free offense we've been leaving on the floor.

It shows up twice more after the identification block: the transition block attacks a **2-3
before it can set**, and formations are split into the man answer and the zone answer.

**The free-throw block is the first thing we've measured.** Best of 10, partner counts, and
**we write down the day's best and the day's worst**. Not to embarrass anyone — to have a
number that exists, so next time there's something to beat. It also buys a breather between
the dribbling warm-up and the live blocks without anybody standing.

**Hail Mary is a real situation, not a novelty.** Full-length inbound with a defender on the
ball *and* a defender on the receiver — the end-of-quarter throw we have never once
practiced. Two stations, so everyone gets reps at all four jobs.

**Carryover from Friday:** bigs still play off their man to help and protect the rim, and
**every shot gets boxed out** — which matters more today, because against a zone the box-out
is nobody's assignment until somebody takes it.

**Court availability:** full court 0–60 (**four rims / both courts**), half court 60–90,
sidelines / no basket 90–120.

## Dribbling drill lists

Pulled out of the table so they're readable on a phone — check them off as you call them.

### Warm-up — down and back

*Full court, 10 min, start of practice. Routines first, then the combos, then live 1v1 —
keep it moving, one or two trips per item.*

**Routines**

- Between the legs
- Under drags
- Cross jabs
- Strongside spins
- Weakside spins
- Hips to drop step
- Speed stops
- Wrap around

**Combos on the move**

- Speed stop → Between the legs
- Speed stop → Between the legs → Wrap around
- Speed stop → Behind the back
- Punch, Tween, Retreat, Cross, Double-Cross
- Under drag, Retreat, Cross

**Live**

- 1v1 freestyle — take the moves against a defender

### Stationary — full bank (12)

*Sideline, 8 min. No subset today — run all twelve.*

- Punch Dribbles (outside foot)
- Punch Dribbles + pocket
- In-Out
- Punch + Snatch
- Punch-Cross
- Hi-Low (3 Arm + 3 Wrist)
- Between Legs (alternating)
- Punch + Between + Behind
- Punch, Tween the legs
- Punch, Tween the legs, Wrap Around
- Punch, Retreat, Cross
- Punch, Retreat, Double Cross

### Combinations

*Sideline, 6 min. Both hands on every one.*

- Speed stop → Between the legs
- Speed stop → Between the legs → Wrap around
- Speed stop → Behind the back
- Punch, Tween, Retreat, Cross, Double-Cross
- Under drag, Retreat, Cross
