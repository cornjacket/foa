# Friday 9/18 — 120 min

**14+ athletes** — our 8 plus at least 6 from the D1 squad.

**The floor after 7:15:** full court is the warm-up scrimmage only. **Every scrimmage after that
runs cross-court, sideline to sideline, with a basket at each end** — so transition is live, both
ends, all night. **Drills use the main basket.**

**No block may park six kids in a line.** Every drill below runs two units alternating reps; every
scrimmage puts whoever is off on a **[sideline station](#sideline-station)** — dribbling, which is
also the only ball-handling in the night since warm-ups are pre-7:00.

**The D1 kids don't know any of our calls** — not SL/SR, not spread, not Flex. Every whiteboard
is for them too, and **put one of ours in every group** who can say the call out loud.

**Warm-ups on the sideline as they arrive, 6:50–7:00** — hips, hamstrings, groin, calves.

## 7:00 — Warm-up scrimmage, Green vs White (15)

**Full court, 5v5, nothing on.** Sub on every made basket. **This is the only full-court block —
get the running in now.**

**Split each squad across both teams** — the D1 kids set the tempo, so spread them. An all-D1
side is a blowout, and a blowout teaches nobody anything.

## 7:15 — Drill: Flex, one make each (15)

**One basket, half court.** Whiteboard 4 min, everyone. **Then two groups of 7: A at the basket,
B on the [sideline station](#sideline-station). Swap at the halfway point** — about 5 min of live
reps each.

**Shooter is the man on the block. Wings must get open.**

- Ball out to wing → **backscreen the strongside wing, he flex-cuts to the opposite block**
- **High post + point stagger away for the weakside low man**, he lifts to the wing
- **Wing passes him the 3**

Options:
1. **Wing drives** — the flex cut emptied the lane
2. **Wing feeds the cutter** at the block
3. **Wing passes to the point**, who takes the stagger for the 3

**Every player needs one make**, miss → next man in. **One basket for 14, so the cap is the clock**
— when the group's time is up, it's up. Full version in [9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 7:30 — Scrimmage: Flex 4v4, SL/SR (15)

**4v4 cross-court, both ends live. 3v3 doesn't run Flex.** 8 on, **6 on the sideline walking the
call** — PG says SL, the block goes to that side, reset, again. That's the rehearsal you wanted,
and it costs no floor time.

**Walk it 3–4 times with the live group first.** The point of the call is that **the block knows
which side to go to before anything else moves.**

Then live. **PG calls SL or SR before the defense is set — no call, no ball.** Last-bucket subs
keep the sideline six rotating in.

## 7:45 — Drill: spread inbound, offense only (12)

**No defense. An elbow make starts it** — ball goes in, the inbounder takes it out, we advance.
**Switch inbound spots every rep.**

**Two units of 5 alternating reps** (third unit of 4 borrows a man). Unit A runs it and clears to
the sideline, **unit B is already going**. Nobody waits more than one rep.

- **Wing open** → pass to strongside wing, **strong elbow cuts diagonal to midcourt**
- **Wing covered** → wing screens for strong elbow, **screener rolls to midcourt**
- **Both** → weakside elbow screens for weakside wing
- **Last resort** → inbounder runs the baseline
- **Zone** → no screens, go to open space

**Why we spread — make them say it back:**
- Bunched up, **one defender guards two men**. Spread, every defender has exactly one job
- **Every man has an escape angle.** Nobody is trapped against a line
- **The diagonal to midcourt is only open if nobody is standing in it**

## 7:57 — Scrimmage: 4v4 spread, sideline to sideline (13)

**Cross-court, score at the far basket.** Same floor as every other scrimmage tonight.

- **Spread the whole way** — no two men in the same lane
- **Passing only until near midcourt** — midcourt here is **halfway between the two sidelines**.
  Dribble is live from there in
- Early dribble → turnover, other way

**Last-bucket subs**, 6 on the station.

## 8:10 — Drill: 5-man transition, 2-3 (15)

Whiteboard, then **walk it with 5, no defense, half speed, both sides.** **Two units of 5
alternating** so the floor is never empty and nobody watches twice in a row.

**Fill 2-3: two up top (ball + trailer), three across (two wings wide, one rim runner).**

Rebound → **kickout** → **pass forward** → **set the screen** → **drive** → **pass to the cutter**.

*Half court, so the break is compressed, not cancelled — the spacing and the order of events are
what we're drilling, not the distance.*

## 8:25 — Scrimmage: 3v3 transition, 2-1 (15)

**Cross-court, both ends live — this is the block the two baskets buy us.** Rebound → **outlet**
→ **advance** → attack the other end **filling 2-1: two up, one in.**

- **Passing only until near midcourt**
- **Outlet goes ahead of the ball**

**Four teams** (two of 3, two of 4 — the 4s rotate a man each possession). Score or stop, next team on.

## 8:40 — 5-Out motion (20)

**5v5 cross-court, 4 off, last-bucket subs.**

- **5 min** — refresher + 5v0 walk-through. Downstream, downstream, zoom
- **5 min** — defense at half speed
- **10 min** — **live, Green vs White.** **Every pass is followed by a cut or a screen** — stand
  still and it's a turnover

## Sideline station

For whoever is off the floor. Down the sideline and back.

**Routines:** between the legs · under drags · cross jabs · strongside spins · weakside spins · hips to drop step · speed stops · wrap around

**Combos:** speed stop → tween · speed stop → tween → wrap · speed stop → behind the back · punch, tween, retreat, cross, double-cross · under drag, retreat, cross

## Subs

**Last scorer and his man come off at the next dead ball.** In the drill blocks the rotation is
the scoring rule — miss and the next man is in.
