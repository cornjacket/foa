# Friday 9/18 — 120 min

**14+ athletes** — our 8 plus at least 6 from the D1 squad. **Full court all night**, two working
baskets. Everything below assumes it; if we lose the far end, cut to one game live and rotate
teams on every score.

**The D1 kids don't know any of our calls** — not SL/SR, not spread, not Flex. Every whiteboard
is for them too, and **put one of ours in every group** who can say the call out loud.

**Warm-ups on the sideline as they arrive, 6:50–7:00** — hips, hamstrings, groin, calves. The
ball goes up at 7:00.

## 7:00 — Warm-up scrimmage, Green vs White (15)

**Full court, 5v5, nothing on.** Sub on every made basket.

**Split each squad across both teams** — the D1 kids set the tempo, so spread them. An all-D1
side is a blowout, and a blowout teaches nobody anything.

## 7:15 — Drill: Flex, one make each (15)

Whiteboard 4 min, everyone. **Then split: two baskets, 7 each** — 5 running the action, 2 waiting.

**Shooter is the man on the block. Wings must get open.**

- Ball out to wing → **backscreen the strongside wing, he flex-cuts to the opposite block**
- **High post + point stagger away for the weakside low man**, he lifts to the wing
- **Wing passes him the 3**

Options:
1. **Wing drives** — the flex cut emptied the lane
2. **Wing feeds the cutter** at the block
3. **Wing passes to the point**, who takes the stagger for the 3

**Every player needs one make.** Miss → next man subs in. Full version in [9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 7:30 — Scrimmage: Flex 4v4, SL/SR (15)

**4v4 — 3v3 doesn't run Flex.** Two games, two baskets.

**Walk it 3–4 times first: PG calls the side, the block goes there, run it.** That is the whole
point of the call — **the block has to know which side to go to before anything else moves.**

Then live. **PG calls SL or SR before the defense is set — no call, no ball.**

## 7:45 — Drill: spread inbound, offense only (12)

**Half court, no defense. An elbow make starts it** — ball goes in, the inbounder takes it out and
we advance. **Switch inbound spots every rep** so everyone takes it out from both baselines.

- **Wing open** → pass to strongside wing, **strong elbow cuts diagonal to midcourt**
- **Wing covered** → wing screens for strong elbow, **screener rolls to midcourt**
- **Both** → weakside elbow screens for weakside wing
- **Last resort** → inbounder runs the baseline
- **Zone** → no screens, go to open space

**Why we spread — make them say it back:**
- Bunched up, **one defender guards two men**. Spread, every defender has exactly one job
- **Every man has an escape angle.** Nobody is trapped against a line
- **The diagonal to midcourt is only open if nobody is standing in it**

## 7:57 — Scrimmage: 4v4 spread, sideline to sideline (13)

**Across the court, no basket.** Win it by getting the ball over the far sideline under control.

- **Spread the whole way** — no two men in the same lane
- **Passing only until near midcourt.** Dribble is live for the last stretch
- Deflection or a dribble too early → turnover, other way

## 8:10 — Drill: 5-man transition, 2-3 (15)

Whiteboard, then **walk it with 5, no defense, both sides**.

**Fill 2-3: two up top (ball + trailer), three across (two wings wide, one rim runner).**

Rebound → **kickout** → **pass forward** → **set the screen** → **drive** → **pass to the cutter**.

## 8:25 — Scrimmage: 3v3 transition, 2-1 (15)

Live, both ends. **Fill 2-1 — two up, one in.**

**Passing only until near midcourt.** Outlet goes ahead of the ball.

**Four teams** (two of 3, two of 4 — the 4s rotate a man each possession). Score or stop, next team on.

## 8:40 — 5-Out motion (20)

- **5 min** — refresher + 5v0 walk-through. Downstream, downstream, zoom
- **5 min** — defense at half speed
- **10 min** — **live 5v5, Green vs White.** **Every pass is followed by a cut or a screen** — stand still and it's a turnover
