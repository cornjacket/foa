## 7:45 — Drill: Flex, one make each (13)

**One basket, half court.** Whiteboard 3 min, everyone. **Then two groups of 7: A at the basket,
B on the [sideline station](#sideline-station). Swap at 7:53** — 5 min of live reps each.

**Shooter is the man on the block. Wings must get open.**

- Ball out to wing → **backscreen the strongside wing, he flex-cuts to the opposite block**
- **High post + point stagger away for the weakside low man**, he lifts to the wing
- **Wing passes him the 3**

Options:
1. **Wing drives** — the flex cut emptied the lane
2. **Wing feeds the cutter** at the block
3. **Wing passes to the point**, who takes the stagger for the 3

**Every player needs one make**, miss → next man in. **One basket for 14, so the cap is the clock**
— when the group's time is up, it's up. Full version in [9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 7:58 — Scrimmage: Flex 4v4, SL/SR (13)

**4v4 cross-court, both ends live. 3v3 doesn't run Flex.** 8 on, **6 on the sideline walking the
call** — PG says SL, the block goes to that side, reset, again.

**Walk it 3 times with the live group first.** The point of the call is that **the block knows
which side to go to before anything else moves.**

Then live. **PG calls SL or SR before the defense is set — no call, no ball.** Last-bucket subs
keep the sideline six rotating in.

## 8:11 — Drill: spread inbound, offense only (11)

**No defense. An elbow make starts it** — ball goes in, the inbounder takes it out, we advance.
**Switch inbound spots every rep.**

**Two units of 5 alternating reps** (third unit of 4 borrows a man). Unit A clears to the
sideline, **unit B is already going**.

- **Wing open** → pass to strongside wing, **strong elbow cuts diagonal to midcourt**
- **Wing covered** → wing screens for strong elbow, **screener rolls to midcourt**
- **Both** → weakside elbow screens for weakside wing
- **Last resort** → inbounder runs the baseline
- **Zone** → no screens, go to open space

**Why we spread — make them say it back:**
- Bunched up, **one defender guards two men**. Spread, every defender has exactly one job
- **Every man has an escape angle.** Nobody is trapped against a line
- **The diagonal to midcourt is only open if nobody is standing in it**

## 8:22 — Scrimmage: 4v4 spread, sideline to sideline (12)

**Cross-court, score at the far basket.**

- **Spread the whole way** — no two men in the same lane
- **Passing only until near midcourt** — midcourt here is **halfway between the two sidelines**.
  Dribble is live from there in
- Early dribble → turnover, other way

**Last-bucket subs**, 6 on the station.

## 8:34 — Drill: 5-man transition, 2-3 (13)

Whiteboard, then **walk it with 5, no defense, half speed, both sides.** **Two units of 5
alternating.**

**Fill 2-3: two up top (ball + trailer), three across (two wings wide, one rim runner).**

Rebound → **kickout** → **pass forward** → **set the screen** → **drive** → **pass to the cutter**.

## 8:47 — Scrimmage: 3v3 transition, 2-1 (13)

**Four teams** (two of 3, two of 4 — the 4s rotate a man each possession). Score or stop, next team on.

**Routines:** between the legs · under drags · cross jabs · strongside spins · weakside spins · hips to drop step · speed stops · wrap around
