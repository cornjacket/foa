# Friday 8/7 — 120 min

**Ten blocks, most of them 10 minutes.** Nothing on the sheet runs long enough to go stale —
the tradeoff is that every block has to be called tight and started on time, because there's
no slack anywhere to absorb a slow start.

**Court runs one direction and never comes back:** full court 0–40, sideline 40–60, half
court 60–120. The half-court stretch runs uninterrupted from the box-out chain straight
through the scrimmage, so nothing has to be reset mid-block.

**We have 10 athletes, so the offense blocks are 5v5 offense vs. defense.** Ten minutes each
— **clear-out / screen away**, then **pick 'n roll** — every athlete on the floor, the
offense read by a live defense instead of air. At 10 minutes apiece these are read-checks,
not installs: run it, fix the read that broke, run it again.

**Today's defensive emphasis: the bigs play off their man.** Sag into the gap, sit between
your man and the ball, and be there to **help on the drive and block the shot** — we'd
rather give up a pass to a big standing 15 feet out than a layup. Two things have to come
with it or it's just a man left open:

1. **Recover on the pass.** Off the ball doesn't mean out of the play — close out short and
   under control when it swings back to your man.
2. **You still have to box out from the gap**, and that's the hard part — when the shot goes
   up you aren't touching anybody, so you have to **find him first, then seal**. That's
   exactly what the 3-person box-out chain drills, and it's the standard in both 5v5 blocks
   and the scrimmage.

**Box out comes over from Tuesday as a pair, in Tuesday's order** — the 1v1 first (nothing
but turn, find, seal, rebound), the 3-person chain later, where the same seal has to survive
a drive, a pass, and a gap rotation. Fix the seal in isolation before asking anyone to do it
at the end of a sequence.

**Press break moves to the sideline and gets small.** No full-court 5v5 press break today —
it's **2v2 inbounding**, inbounder and one receiver vs. two defenders, coach counting the 5
out loud. 5v5 is too crowded to see who's wrong; 2v2 strips it to the read. That's also
where the zone-press question finally gets a straight answer.

**Zoom is the bridge into the 5v5 blocks.** Three lines, no defense — rehearse the reads
(**zoom, roll, fade, pass, one more**) at speed and on air, then walk into the live blocks
where a defense decides which one you get. Roll and fade are the same two reads the pick 'n
roll block tests 20 minutes later.

**Cut from 7/31:** the Triangle inbound and the standalone 1v1 off the Downscreen / Clear
Out — that read now lives inside the first 5v5 block, against a defense trying to take it
away. **The stationary dribbling bank also stays out**; Tuesday ran all twelve three days
ago, and moving ball-handling keeps its 10 up front.

**Court availability:** full court 0–40, sideline / no basket 40–60, half court 60–120.

*Full court, 10 min, to open. Routines first, then the combos, then live 1v1 — keep it
moving, one trip per item, don't linger. No stationary bank today; Tuesday ran all twelve.*

- Between the legs
- Under drags
- Cross jabs
- Strongside spins
- Weakside spins
- Hips to drop step
- Speed stops
- Wrap around

- Speed stop → Between the legs
- Speed stop → Between the legs → Wrap around
- Speed stop → Behind the back
- Punch, Tween, Retreat, Cross, Double-Cross
- Under drag, Retreat, Cross

**Live**

- 1v1 freestyle — take the moves against a defender
