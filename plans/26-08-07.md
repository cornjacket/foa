# Friday 8/7 — 120 min

**Friday 7/31's plan with the box-out work rebuilt from Tuesday and the back half turned
over to 5-Out offense vs. defense.** Same 120 minutes, same court flow as 7/31 — sideline
to open, full court for the press break, half court the rest of the way.

**We have 10 athletes, so the back half is 5v5.** That's the whole reason the plan changes
shape today: two 20-minute offense/defense blocks — **clear-out / screen away**, then
**pick 'n roll** — where every athlete is on the floor the entire time and the offense is
being read by a live defense instead of air. Nobody stands.

**Box out comes over from Tuesday as a pair, in Tuesday's order.** The 1v1 first — nothing
but turn, find, seal, rebound — then the 3-person chain right behind it, where the same seal
has to survive a drive, a pass, and a gap rotation. Tuesday's lesson holds: fix the seal in
isolation before asking anyone to do it at the end of a sequence.

**Today's defensive emphasis: the bigs play off their man.** Sag into the gap, sit between
your man and the ball, and be there to **help on the drive and block the shot** — we'd
rather give up a pass to a big standing 15 feet out than a layup. Two things have to come
with it or it's just a man left open:

1. **Recover on the pass.** Off the ball doesn't mean out of the play — close out short and
   under control when it swings back to your man.
2. **You still have to box out from the gap**, and that's the hard part — when the shot goes
   up you aren't touching anybody, so you have to **find him first, then seal**. That's
   exactly what the 3-person box-out chain drills, and it's the standard in both 5v5 blocks
   and the scrimmage.

**Zoom is the bridge into the 5v5 blocks.** Three lines, no defense — rehearse the reads
(**zoom, roll, fade, pass, one more**) at speed and on air, then walk straight into the two
live blocks where a defense decides which one you get. The roll and the fade are the same
two reads the pick 'n roll block tests 30 minutes later.

**Cut from 7/31:** the 2-3 Transition Drill, the 1v1 off the Downscreen / Clear Out, and the
Triangle. The downscreen/clear-out read isn't gone — it's the first 5v5 block, run live
against a defense that's actually trying to take it away. **The stationary dribbling bank
also comes out** to make room for Zoom — moving ball-handling still gets its 15 up front,
and Tuesday ran the whole stationary bank three days ago.

**Court availability:** sideline / no basket 0–15, full court 15–40, half court 40–120.

*Sideline, 15 min, to open. Routines first, then the combos, then live 1v1 — keep it moving,
one or two trips per item. No stationary bank today; Tuesday ran all twelve.*

- Under drags
- Cross jabs
- Strongside spins
- Weakside spins
- Hips to drop step
- Speed stops
- Wrap around

**Combos on the move**

- Speed stop → Between the legs
- Speed stop → Between the legs → Wrap around
- Speed stop → Behind the back
- Punch, Tween, Retreat, Cross, Double-Cross
- Under drag, Retreat, Cross

**Live**

- 1v1 freestyle — take the moves against a defender
