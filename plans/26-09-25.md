**After that we're on half court with our 8 or 9, and every scrimmage is 4v4.** **Drills use the
main basket.** **Scrimmages run cross-court, sideline to sideline, with a basket at each end** — so

## 7:30 — Warm-up scrimmage, Green vs White (15)

**Full court, 5v5. D2 vs D1** — Green gets reps against the better team.

**Five of ours on, 3 or 4 off** — this is the one block tonight with a real bench, so run them

**Constraint: on a made basket, pass it out of the backcourt** — no dribble until near half court.

**At 7:45 we go to half court.** Split our 8 into the two fours that stay together the rest of the
night.

## 7:45 — Drill: Flex, one make each (13)

**One basket, half court.** Whiteboard 3 min, everyone. **Then 5 on the floor, the rest waiting.**

**Shooter is the man on the block. Wings must get open.**

- Ball out to wing → **backscreen the strongside wing, he flex-cuts to the opposite block**
- **High post + point stagger away for the weakside low man**, he lifts to the wing
- **Wing passes him the 3**

Options:
1. **Wing drives** — the flex cut emptied the lane
2. **Wing feeds the cutter** at the block
3. **Wing passes to the point**, who takes the stagger for the 3

**Every player needs one make** — 8 or 9 makes ends it. **Miss → next man subs in.** If the makes
aren't there by 7:58, it's over anyway — the clock is the cap. Full version in [9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 7:58 — Scrimmage: Flex 4v4, SL/SR (13)

**4v4 cross-court, both ends live.**

**Walk it 3 times first, both teams standing in it.** The point of the call is that **the block knows
which side to go to before anything else moves.**

Then live. **PG calls SL or SR before the defense is set — no call, no ball.**

**Flex here is the backscreen and nothing else** — no downscreen, no stagger, no 3. Run the flex
cut, then take **option 1 (drive when the lane clears)** or **option 2 (feed the cutter at the
block)**. The 3 off the stagger lives in the drill, not this game.

## 8:11 — Drill: spread inbound, offense only (11)

**No defense. An elbow make starts it** — ball goes in, the inbounder takes it out, we advance.
**Switch inbound spots every rep.**

**One unit of 5 at a time; the other 3 or 4 are the next unit and borrow a man.** Unit A clears to
the sideline, **unit B is already going.**

- **Wing open** → pass to strongside wing, **strong elbow cuts diagonal to midcourt**
- **Wing covered** → wing screens for strong elbow, **screener rolls to midcourt**
- **Both** → weakside elbow screens for weakside wing
- **Last resort** → inbounder runs the baseline
- **Zone** → no screens, go to open space

**Why we spread — make them say it back:**
- Bunched up, **one defender guards two men**. Spread, every defender has exactly one job
- **Every man has an escape angle.** Nobody is trapped against a line
- **The diagonal to midcourt is only open if nobody is standing in it**

## 8:22 — Scrimmage: 4v4 spread, sideline to sideline (12)

**Cross-court, score at the far basket.**

- **Spread the whole way** — no two men in the same lane
- **Passing only until near midcourt** — midcourt here is **halfway between the two sidelines**.
  Dribble is live from there in
- Early dribble → turnover, other way

## 8:34 — Drill: 5-man transition, 2-3 (13)

Whiteboard, then **walk it with 5, no defense, half speed, both sides.** The others watch, then
swap in for a trip each way.

**Fill 2-3: two up top (ball + trailer), three across (two wings wide, one rim runner).**

Rebound → **kickout** → **pass forward** → **set the screen** → **drive** → **pass to the cutter**.

## 8:47 — Scrimmage: 4v4 transition, 2-2 (13)

**Cross-court, both ends live.** Rebound → **outlet** → **advance** → attack the other end.

**Four men fill 2-2: ball and trailer up top, one wing wide, one rim runner.** Same shape as the
5-man break with the second wing taken out — **the rim runner does not disappear because we're a
man short.**

- **Passing only until near midcourt**
- **Outlet goes ahead of the ball**
