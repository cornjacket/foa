**Full court, 5v5. D2 vs D1** — Green gets reps against the better team.

**Five of ours on, 3 or 4 off** — this is the one block tonight with a real bench, so run them

**Constraint: on a made basket, pass it out of the backcourt** — no dribble until near half court.

**At 7:45 we go to half court.**

## 7:45 — 5-Out, half court (15)

**Main basket, offense only.** Walk it, then up to speed. Everybody spreads.

- **To zoom**
- **To motion** — **screen away** or **drag screen**

**Run both exits off the same 5-Out look** so the shape doesn't tell the defense which one is
coming.

## 8:00 — Scrimmage: 5-on-few, half court (15)

**5v3 at 8, 5v4 at 9.** Main basket.

**Every possession starts with the offense: inbound out of X, bring the ball up, then run it.**
No pickup ball — **it's 5-Out or motion or it's a turnover.**

## 8:15 — Drill: spread inbound after a make (15)

**Offense is down a man — 3v4 at 8, 4v5 at 9.**

**A made basket starts it** — the scoring team's man takes it out, the inbounding team advances.
**Switch inbound spots every rep.**

**Run it on both floors:**

- **Wide court (first 7)** — baseline to half court, the normal look. Full width to spread into.
  Get the shape right here.
- **Short court (last 8)** — sideline to sideline, basket at each end. **Same drill, less room.**

**Shrinking the floor is the difficulty, and that's the point.** Sideline to sideline there is less
room to spread, the help is closer, and a man short means **every escape angle has to be real.**
If they can do it on the short court, the wide one is easy.

## 8:30 — Drill: Flex formation (15)

**Main basket. Live defenders on the two wings, nobody else guarded** — that keeps the drill
honest. **The wings have to get open**; without a man on them the whole formation is just walking
through spots. **5 offense, 2 on the wings, the rest waiting. Rotate the defenders every four
reps** so nobody guards all block.

Get all four finishes on the board — **run it until each one has been scored**:

1. **The block scores the 3**
2. **The weakside wing scores**
3. **The wing drives and scores lefty**
4. **The wing drives and kicks back to the point for the 3**

Full version of the formation in [9/11](26-09-11.md).

## 8:45 — Scrimmage: 4v4 spread inbound, short court (15)

**Sideline to sideline, both ends live. Even teams, 4v4.**

**After a made basket: 5 seconds to get it in, 5 more to cross half court. Beat both and it's a
point.** Half court here is **halfway between the two sidelines**.

**I am the clock, and my five is the five.** Count it out loud so they play to the count, not to a
scoreboard.

**Once across, the PG calls SL or SR before the defense is set — no call, no ball.** The call is
the whole point: **the block knows which side to go to before anything else moves.**

**At 4v4 that's the backscreen and the flex cut, not the full formation** — the stagger and the 3
need five men and live in the drill.

hurt, sitting out, or waiting a long stretch. Down the sideline and back.
