# Friday 10/30 — 120 min

**7 players expected. 7:00–7:30 is warm-ups, dribbling and 1v1. Half-court time is 7:30–9:00.**
**Tonight is form shooting, then inbounds, then the half-court sets, then a 3v3 scrimmage.**

**With 7 players:** use 3 lines or 3 baskets of 3, 2 and 2. Every 5-on-0 block has 2 players ready
to sub in, so sub often and keep the waits short.

---

## Warm-ups and 1v1 (7:00–7:30)

### 7:00 — Warm-up (5)

Easy jog, then hips, hamstrings, groin, calves.

### 7:05 — Dribbling patterns (13)

Down and back. **3 lines: 3, 2 and 2.**

**Routines:** between the legs · under drags · cross jabs · speed stops

**Combos (last 5 min):** speed stop → tween · speed stop → tween → wrap · under drag, retreat, cross

### 7:18 — 1v1 sideline to sideline (12)

Three pairs go at once. The seventh player steps in for the next rep, so a different player sits
each time. Swap offense and defense on every rep.

---

## Half court (7:30–9:00)

### 7:30 — Huddle (2)

Set the 3 basket groups: 3, 2 and 2.

### 7:32 — Form shooting: close, mid, far (8)

**Perfect form. No sloppy 3s — stay in your range.** Make a few from close, then move to mid, then
far. A player's far spot is the edge of their own range, not the 3-point line.

### 7:40 — Quick shooting off a pass (7)

**Chest-level pass, quick shot, with a closeout.** The passer closes out on the shooter after every
pass. Shooter and passer swap on every rep.

### 7:47 — 1v1 to 5 points (8)

**Game to 5. The left hand wins the game:** the winning point must be a left-hand finish. The group
of 3 plays winner stays.

### 7:55 — Water (2)

Bring everyone to one basket.

### 7:57 — Triangle (8)

5 on 0, with 2 players ready to sub in. Call it out loud, then run it.

- **Shooters inbound** — a shooter takes the ball out
- **Elevator**
- **Post up**

### 8:05 — Spread (8)

5 on 0, with 2 players ready to sub in. **Get into Spread right away.** We did not do it immediately
last time, and the coach had to waste a timeout.

- **Half court** (4)
- **Sideline to sideline** (4)

### 8:13 — Flex, 3-point play (12)

**Everyone gets 3 shots.** After a player's third shot, that player subs out and a waiting player
subs in. Keep going until all 7 have taken 3 shots.

### 8:25 — 2-1-2, X formation (12)

5 on 0, with 2 players ready to sub in. Walk each action, then run it at full speed.

- **Move the ball around, move to the ball carrier**
- **Screen and roll to a wheel**
- **Zoom** — the high post needs to vacate
- **Split** — open up the 3

### 8:37 — 5-Out motion (8)

5 on 0, with 2 players ready to sub in.

1. **First, a downscreen**
2. **Then clear out or screen away**

### 8:45 — 3v3 scrimmage (12)

3v3, with 1 player ready to sub in. Sub after every possession so nobody sits for long.

### 8:57 — Huddle (3)

Who called Spread right away. Next week's schedule.
