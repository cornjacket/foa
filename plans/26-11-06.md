### 7:42 — Form shooting: elbow to elbow, quick shot in stride (7)

**3 baskets, 3 shooters, 3 rebounders.** The shooter runs from one elbow to the other. The
rebounder throws a **high pass**, and the shooter catches in stride and takes a quick shot. The
rebounder gets the ball and passes again as the shooter goes back the other way. Shooter and
rebounder swap halfway.

### 7:49 — Behind-the-back, high off the glass layups (5)

**2 baskets, sideline to sideline, 2 drivers.** Behind-the-back dribble into a high-off-the-glass
layup. Rebound your own shot, then **go across** and attack the other basket. Use both hands.

### 7:54 — Layup chase from behind (5)

**Full speed, sideline to sideline.** The driver goes for the layup. **I call the chaser**, who
starts behind the driver and tries to catch up and contest. The driver finishes at full speed.

## Transition (8:13–8:25)

### 8:13 — Transition into Spread (6)

Inbound to the wing. The wing passes forward to the **slanting elbow**. The wing then sets a
screen, and the elbow drives. Finish with a **jump shot**, or the **inbounder trails for the
layup**. *Option:* add a defense once it runs clean.

### 8:19 — 2+2+2 transition Pick'n Pop (6)

**3 pairs: wing and high post, X formation.** Inbound to the wing, pass back to the inbounder.
Run down the court into a **Pick'n Pop**. The popper reads it: **pump and drive**, or take the
**open 3**. Pairs go one after another.
