# Friday 7/24 — 120 min

Picks up directly from **Tuesday 7/21**. Tuesday introduced the **5-Out Clear Out**
read progression and the **Triangle** sideline inbound. Today we run both back to
lock in the reads, and bring the **Press Break** back in.

**Only 5 players today.** That shapes the whole session — no 4v4, no 5-man shell.
The good news: **5 players is exactly right for the Clear Out drill**, so it runs
full-team with everyone in a real spot. Defense work drops to **3v2 help defense**,
and the scrimmage runs **3v2 with a rotating extra**.

**Warm-up is 30 min** — stationary dribbling into the down-and-back package.

**Court availability:** sideline / no basket to start (dribbling), full court for
Press Break, half court the remainder.
