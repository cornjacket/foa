## Green half court (7:50–9:00)

**High effort, with defense in every block.** **Every half-court scrimmage is 3v3** — sub in after
each possession so nobody sits for long.

### 7:50 — Rebound → Spread → transition (8)

5 on 0, full speed.

1. **Rebound** the make (or the miss), step out and **inbound in Spread** — no immediate dribble
2. **Push it forward** into transition
3. **Trailer sets the drag screen** for the ball handler

Run it until every player has been the inbounder and the trailer.

### 7:58 — Inbound plays (8)

Call it out loud, then run it. Rotate the inbounder.

- **Sideline: Triangle** — feed the big on the block, inbounder cuts to the corner for the 3
- **Baseline: X · Stack-1 · Stack-2 · Spread** — no immediate dribble on Spread

### 8:06 — Modified Flex (downscreen start) (16)

1. **Walk each action, 5 on 0** (6) — all four start with the downscreen.
   - **Flex** — the base action
   - **Split action**
   - **Flex zoom** — **the high post must go to the corner**
   - **Flex wheel (drag)**
2. **Every player gets one make** (10) — coach calls the action, and the Flex runs until each
   player has scored once.
   - **Defense guards the wings only** (two defenders). Nobody guards the point or the posts, so the
     screens get clean reps. The wings have to use the screens to get open.
   - **No forced 3s.** A rushed or contested 3 does not count, and the possession resets.
   - Rotate the scorers and the two wing defenders in so nobody sits for long.

### 8:22 — 3v2 drive-kick-swing (8)

Offense (3) vs defense (2). **Drive, kick, swing for 3.** Defense helps on the drive, then recovers
to the shooter on the kick-out. Rotate after every possession.

### 8:30 — Pack-line defense, 4v4 (10)

**Offense passes 10 times, then it is live.** Defense stays inside the pack line, helps on every
pass and closes out when the ball goes live.

*Option:* **4v3** to put more pressure on the defense's rotations.

### 8:40 — 4v4 Corners vs pack-line (15)

**Corners without the strong-side corner.** Offense works:

- **Downscreen**
- **Pick-n-roll**

**Defense plays pack-line and switches the screens.** Same Corners rules as the scrimmage: 10
passes, no immediate dribble, giving up the dribble is a turnover.
