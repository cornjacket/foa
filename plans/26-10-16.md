## Green half court (7:50–9:00)

**Every block is a high-effort defensive drill, then a small-sided game that uses the same skill
right away.** No rest between the two.

### 7:50 — Closeouts → 3v3 drive-kick-swing (18)

1. **Closeout drill** (8) — chop steps, high hands, contest without fouling. 3 lines of 3.
2. **3v3 live** (10) — a basket counts only after a drive-and-kick or a swing. Defense closes out on
   every kick. Winners stay on.

### 8:08 — Modified Flex (downscreen start) (20)

1. **Walk it, 5 on 0** (4) — starts with the downscreen.
2. **Every player gets one make** (16) — run the Flex until each player has scored once.
   - **Defense guards the wings only** (two defenders). Nobody guards the point or the posts, so the
     screens get clean reps. The wings have to use the downscreen and the flex screen to get open.
   - **No forced 3s.** A rushed or contested 3 does not count, and the possession resets.
   - Rotate the scorers and the two wing defenders in so nobody sits for long.

### 8:28 — Help defense → 4v4 Corners (20)

1. **3v2 help defense** (8) — defense rotates on the drive, then recovers to the shooter on the
   kick-out.
2. **4v4 Corners** (12) — the same rules as the scrimmage: 10 passes, no immediate dribble, give up
   the dribble = turnover, finish with drive, kick and swing for 3. Defense uses the rotation it just
   drilled.
