# Friday 8/14 — 120 min

Sidelines 0–30, half court 30–120. The half-court hour and a half is **Tuesday 8/11's
half-court work expanded** — chase layup, free throws, the five 5-Out actions, and the same
scrimmage rule. Tuesday's press identification and full-court inbound need a floor we don't
have; the inbound work comes back as the **baseline 1-4 spread**, which is a half-court play
and the one we'll actually call in a game. **Hail Mary is out** — no full court, no drill.

**One basket, so free throws are a whole-team rep, not a line.** Shooter at the stripe,
everybody else in box-out slots, misses are live. Two things we need at once.

Gap-help defense doesn't get its own block today. It gets drilled inside the box-out chain,
inside the zone inbound, and live in the scrimmage.

If they switch, the mismatch cuts. Second look: flare to the corner.<br>**vs. zone — box out.** Nobody to screen, so **seal the zone**: find the defender covering the spot you want, put your back in his chest, teammate flashes into the gap he can't leave. - Under drags
