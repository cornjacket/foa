# Friday 8/14 — 120 min

Sidelines 0–30, half court 30–120. The half-court hour and a half is **Tuesday 8/11's
half-court work expanded** — chase layup, the five 5-Out actions, and the same scrimmage
rule. Tuesday's full-court blocks (Hail Mary, press identification, full-court inbound)
don't survive the floor we have, so the one piece of them that lives on a half court —
**drive and kick out to the wing** — comes over on its own.

| Time | Block | Detail | Court |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 min | Dribbling warm-up | Down and back, then combos — [list](#warm-up--down-and-back) | Sideline |
| 6 min | Stretching | Dynamic while they're moving, static once the legs are warm. Hips, hamstrings, groin, calves, shoulders | Sideline |
| 12 min | 1v1 containment | Sideline to sideline. Cut him off, chest to chest, no reach. Force him backward or sideways — a change of direction is a win for the defense. Rotate handler → defender → back of line | Sideline |
| 10 min | Chase layup | L side / R side, R hand / L hand | Half court |
| 15 min | Drive → kickout to wing | Tuesday's transition finish, half court. Attack the paint, make the help commit, take the layup if it's there — otherwise kick back out to the wing. On air first, then 2 defenders back so the drive is a read | Half court |
| 20 min | Offensive formations | 5-Out: immediate DHO, immediate Zoom, Zoom on 2nd downscreen, Motion screen away, Motion P'n'R. Walk each one, then run all five against a live defense | Half court |
| 10 min | Box out — 3 person | Of1 drives outside or spins inside → passes to Of2 → De1 drops to the gap → Of2 shoots → De1 seals Of1. Find him first, then seal — from the gap you aren't touching anybody when the shot goes up | Half court |
| 10 min | Gap defense — 5-man shell | Sag between your man and the ball, help on the drive, recover short on the pass, box out from the gap. Paint-only scoring — makes them collapse and rotate | Half court |
| 25 min | Scrimmage | Scorer and his defender sub out. Every possession runs one: P'n'R, DHO, Zoom, screen away. Box out every shot — a missed seal is the only thing we stop play for | Half court |
