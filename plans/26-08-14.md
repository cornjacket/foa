# Friday 8/14 — 120 min

Sidelines 0–30, half court 30–120. The half-court hour and a half is **Tuesday 8/11's
half-court work expanded** — chase layup, free throws, the five 5-Out actions, and the same
scrimmage rule. Tuesday's press identification and full-court inbound need a floor we don't
have; the inbound work comes back as the **baseline 1-4 spread**, which is a half-court play
and the one we'll actually call in a game. **Hail Mary is out** — no full court, no drill.

**The inbound block is a reading drill, run in three tiers.** They break into the 1-4 out of
the key crossing sides, name the defense before the ball is slapped, and face all four looks
— **2 in the backcourt, man press, 1-2-1-1, 2-2-1** — at each tier. Standing defense first,
then half speed, both finishing **drive and kick out to the trailer**; at full speed the
coverage stops being announced and they have to identify it themselves. Scoring counts
**passes, not baskets**, so the rep doesn't end the moment the ball is inbounded.

Tiers are ~5 minutes apiece, which is two or three reps per defense per tier. **If it's
running short, the standing tier is where all four looks have to get named** — that's the
identification rep. Half and full speed can drop to the two that gave us trouble.

**Free throws run on all 3 baskets** — three groups, shooter plus rebounders, so nobody
waits through nine other athletes to shoot. Triple the reps on the thing we're worst at.

**The offense block is a ladder, not a list of formations.** PG alone, then 3-person DHO,
then 4–5 person, then the same two rungs for Zoom, then 5-person motion — one body added
per stage, so the same handoff gets read with more traffic each time instead of being
installed cold at five. **The standalone drive → kickout block is gone**; that read is
stage 1, off the PG's own drive, which is where it comes from anyway.

Gap-help defense doesn't get its own block today. It gets drilled inside the box-out chain,
inside the zone inbound, and live in the scrimmage.
